# Expanded hot single-window continuation, version 2

This is a new controller and contract. Frozen version 1, its failed temporal
screen, the budget-prefix failure and all original candidates remain intact.
The physical candidate, acceptance helper, PySPH backend, initial independent
918-particle model, complete energy history, material/contact laws, parameter
values and all numerical gates are unchanged. No inverse-solver optimization
is introduced.

Version 2 adds coarse-step choices 1.953125 and 3.90625 microseconds to the old
7.8125, 15.625, 31.25, 62.5 and 125 microsecond choices. Fine is always half
coarse and growth is at most four times the preceding accepted fine step.
Every version-2 window must bind the exact independently audited bounded
contact-onset study receipt with SHA
a0a9c0b99c86c6f2b02182bbc5320f87231296464d1afe1539515edde137a67f,
its frozen reader and complete source/evidence map. The eight-versus-four
substep comparison passed every registered shared endpoint; the four-versus-two
comparison still failed. This is local numerical evidence, not a general
time-step guarantee, physical validation, monotone convergence of every field,
or permission to publish diagnostic states.

The study used the accepted 62.5 microsecond/global-step-14 parent, preserved
its complete ledger and ended at 70.3125 microseconds. Every diagnostic remains
unaccepted and nonrestartable. Actual process work must solve again from an
actual accepted fine checkpoint with an independently audited exact endpoint.
The first proposed process window uses that true 62.5 microsecond parent,
coarse 1.953125 microseconds and at most three pairs, ending at 68.359375
microseconds if all three comparisons pass. Publication requires each original
same-parent global/local/observer screen. Independent saved-only readback is
required after the process and before selecting its checkpoint as a new parent.

Version 2 includes every complete candidate time and whole-stage wall/count
from both four- and eight-step diagnostics in every budget, including the
temporally failed four-step diagnostic. It also includes the actual parent
window measurements. These are a cost floor; later actual process costs may
increase them. The maximum study candidate cost was 41.2291884 seconds, so a
six-candidate fine stage forecasts approximately 410.063 seconds using
30 + 1.5 * candidate_count * (maximum_measured_cost + 1 second). No old faster
parent-only estimate may replace this study cost.

Pre-coarse pair selection, immutable protocol, pre-fine qualified-prefix budget
selection, explicit unused reference listing, single-window commands, complete
source hashes and failure preservation follow the frozen version-1 protocol.
Coarse and fine forecasts must each be at most 450 seconds. Each stage is
single-threaded with a 540-second internal and 600-second external cap. No
automatic repeated halving, unbounded campaign, energy reset or tolerance
relaxation is authorized. Repeated unresolved temporal or physical failure
requires method review rather than indefinite small-step accumulation.
